EXPERIMENT RULES

How this experiment works

1

Draw a true size a, add judgment error, and clip the perceived size z to [0, 1].

2

Choose Hard with probability g(z), or Simple otherwise, then calculate the final reported size r. The true size is not consulted again.

3

The algorithm places r using effective capacity G + τ. A single r > G + τ occupies its own bin; physical reported capacity remains 1.

4

Each bin costs 1 to open, plus β for every unit of reported fill above G. True-size overflow is not checked or charged.

5

First Fit uses the earliest fitting bin; Best Fit the fullest; Worst Fit the emptiest; Next Fit only its current bin. Harmonic (K = 10) separates sizes into classes.
